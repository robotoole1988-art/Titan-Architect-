## The diagnosis in one paragraph

Substance averages ~8.6; staging averages ~6. TITAN is a decades-ahead engine
wearing a dev-tool's clothes. Every point of the gap is presentation-shaped —
naming, hierarchy, restraint, staging of intelligence — and none of it
requires touching the spine, the artifact law, or the gates. This is the
cheap kind of gap, and it closes screen by screen.

## Definition of "100% ready" (so it ends)

The audit is DONE when: every audited surface scores ≥8 staging on the
founder's eye; no test artifact steers any operating surface; the Law holds on
every screen; and the unaudited list is empty. That is a finite list, not a
feeling — burn it down, then stop polishing and go sell.
