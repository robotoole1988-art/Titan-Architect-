# TITAN Trade Playbooks — Volume 2
### Every trade in the intake dropdown: conversion patterns, UK legal MUSTs, generator LAWS
Date: 2026-07-26 · Companion to the Site Excellence Dossier (Vol 1: universal front-end + conversion laws). Five research tracks, ~50 additional primary sources. MUST = legal/regulatory, non-negotiable. LAW = generator rule.

## Synthesis — what the whole sweep proved

1. **Accreditation badges are DATA, not images.** Every UK scheme (Gas Safe, NICEIC, PCA, HETAS, REFCOM, MCS, FENSA, SPATA, NCCA, NASC, TrustMark, Which? TT, SRA) follows the same three rules: current members only, unmodified official artwork, prefer the dynamic/verifiable embed. Falsely displaying ANY scheme logo breaches the Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regs 2008. → TITAN builds ONE badge registry: scheme, membership number, expiry date, official asset/embed, register deep-link. No verified number = no badge, and copy auto-strips "approved/certified" claims.
2. **Three funnel archetypes cover all ~35 trades** — build as configurable quote modules:
   - **Calculator/lead-capture** (carpet rooms, waste van-load fraction, solar/battery savings, conservatory wizard, boiler quote engine)
   - **Reg-plate / instant fixed price** (automotive: MOT, servicing, wetbelt)
   - **Photo-quote via WhatsApp/upload** (clearance, detailing, damp, emergency roof damage)
3. **Numbers beat logos everywhere**: licence numbers on the homepage (ArbAC AC2467 pattern), insurance figures stated in £m, Companies House + VAT numbers in footer, register deep-links with "check us — and check anyone else" copy.
4. **Rogue-trader counter-positioning is a family-wide lever** (tarmac, tree surgery, clearance, roofing): "We never cold-call. Written quotes only." + duty-of-care education. Almost no incumbent does it explicitly.
5. **Unclaimed guarantees recur** — brickwork workmanship, exterior-cleaning regrowth, artificial-grass installation: TITAN default-inserts a written guarantee block per trade.
6. **Season-flip module** needed once, with per-trade copy packs (spring surge / winter counter-pitch; HVAC even swaps its GBP primary category by season).
7. **The compliance linter pays twice in regulated trades**: SRA-mandated price pages and Gas Safe numbers are simultaneously the highest-converting content. Disclosure modules = conversion features.
8. **GBP primary category is the #1 local ranking factor** (Whitespark) — each trade template ships a recommended primary + secondaries mapping, re-audited every 6 months.

# TRACK A — Emergency & Compliance-Led Home Trades

## Plumbing & Heating (emergency)
- Gas work requires Gas Safe registration; displaying the logo unregistered is a criminal offence (£1,500 fine precedent). Official logo files only, min 12mm, reg number recommended beneath.
- Emergency = speed-proof arms race: "1 hour response" (Pimlico), "no call-out charge, pay only for time on site" (Aspect). Pricing is hourly (£40–60/hr, £100–120 emergency call-out, published rate tables by time band).
- LAWS: gas pages + logo gated on verified Gas Safe number · never recreate the logo · emergency hero variant with response promise + separate /emergency-plumber-{town} pages · hourly rate table (standard/evening/weekend), never fixed repair prices · Emergency (call-only) vs Planned (form allowed) IA split · stopcock first-steps micro-content on emergency pages.

## Boiler Installation
- Benchmark commissioning validates warranty; installer tiers gate warranty claims (Worcester Accredited/Vaillant Advance/Baxi Approved → up to 10yr; Glow-worm to 15yr). Never claim "10-year guarantee" without verified tier.
- BOXT/Heatable defined the category: 8–12 click-only questions → postcode → instant fixed prices for 3–4 tiers → save-quote email capture → 0% finance → next-day install. No salesperson, no phone call.
- LAWS: quote-engine multi-step form (click answers; end with "fixed quote within 2 hours" if no auto-pricing) · 3 fixed package tiers "from £X,XXX installed", never POA · warranty claim data-driven from verified tier · compliance strip "Gas Safe {number} · Benchmark commissioned · warranty registered for you" · finance module only if real · save/email-my-quote step (1–4 week consideration cycle).

## HVAC / Air Conditioning
- F-Gas company certification is a legal requirement for split-system work (REFCOM dominant, verifiable number).
- Master pattern: dual-season repositioning ("cool in summer, efficient heating in winter") kills the summer-only demand curve; fixed-price kW/room-size tiers (Heatable/SelectAir "from £37/month").
- LAWS: REFCOM/F-Gas number required field, rendered in trust strip · year-round hero copy, never cooling-only · room-size price tiers with free-survey confirmation · summer+winter content pair per page for dual-intent SEO · benefit chips beyond temperature (filtration/allergies, dehumidify, dB) · monthly finance framing where real.

## Electricians
- Part P: notifiable domestic work self-certified via Competent Person Scheme (NICEIC/NAPIT); logos licensed to current members only; link "check our registration" to the scheme's public register.
- Landlord EICRs mandatory every 5 years (fines to £30,000) = the demand engine. EICR sold as fixed-price commodity banded by bedrooms ("from £69–79"), same-day certificate; fuseboard replacement = second fixed-price lander (£450–800 guide).
- LAWS: scheme + number required, badge + register link · always generate /eicr-{town} landlord page (bedroom-banded fixed prices, £30k-fine urgency, landlord-pack bundle) · always generate /fuseboard-replacement-{town} · three-lane IA: Emergency (hourly, call-only) / Certificates (fixed menu, book online) / Installations (survey) · every page states the paperwork issued (EICR, EIC, Minor Works) · "Part P registered" chip.

## Damp Proofing
- No licence; PCA membership is the standard (CSRT/CSSW surveyors, insurance-backed guarantees via GPI). Lenders require PCA reports for retentions.
- Survey-first funnel universal (Peter Cox: book-online survey with £25 discount; Timberwise: free homeowner survey vs paid pre-purchase Damp & Timber Report). Symptom-led page architecture (rising damp / penetrating / condensation & mould / dry rot / woodworm).
- LAWS: primary CTA always "Book a survey" (+optional online-booking incentive; photo upload field) · PCA chip gated on membership · guarantee block: N-year written + IBG "protects you if we cease trading" · symptom-tree pages with photo diagnosis cues · two survey products (free homeowner vs paid pre-purchase report) · never price treatments online; 3-step survey→report→itemised quote graphic.

## Chimney & Fireplaces
- Stove installs notifiable (Part J): HETAS installers self-certify ("no council fees"); smoke-control areas need DEFRA-exempt stoves. Sweeping: insurers require annual certificates from recognised bodies (Guild/ICS/APICS/NACS/HETAS) — the certificate IS the product.
- LAWS: two lanes, two funnels — sweeping (fixed price per flue, instant online booking) vs stove install (gallery + free survey) · sweep pages lead with insurance angle + same-day digital certificate · HETAS chip gated · annual-reminder capture (retention engine) · smoke-control-area note auto-inserted by town · seasonal "beat the autumn rush" banner May–Aug.

# TRACK B — Big-Ticket Considered Purchases

## Builders (General) + Extensions & Renovations
- FMB = genuinely vetted (12+ months trading, credit checks, on-site inspection) and a TrustMark route — say the vetting mechanics, not just the logo. State that the firm handles planning + Building Regs (top anxiety).
- #1 organic lead asset: regional cost-per-m² guide pages (£1,650–£4,200/m² banded). Design-and-build converts on fixed price + structured timeline (Plus Rooms), productised journey pages (Simply Extend). Budget BANDS on case studies beat exact prices and no prices. Drone/timelapse = highest-credibility proof.
- LAWS: FMB vetting mechanics stated · cost-per-m² guide page per site with mid-page capture · every project card: location, type, budget band, weeks · numbered build-journey page with week counts · "we handle planning & building control" explicit · staged-payment transparency + deposit/IBG protection · CTA = free site consultation · one timelapse/drone video per flagship case study.

## Windows & Doors (Double Glazing) + Conservatories
- Replacement windows legally require Building Regs compliance — FENSA OR Certass (frame around house-sale risk: "certificate lodged automatically"). FENSA mandates approved IBG providers (QANW/HomePro). Finance promotion = FCA credit-broking territory: CONC 3 representative example required; gate ALL finance copy on an FCA FRN.
- Guarantee length is the headline differentiator (10 vs 15 vs 20 years). Everest-style upfront guide pricing beats opacity for local firms. ConservatoryLand's instant quote engine (style→size→spec→estimate) is the volume pattern; locals approximate with a 3-step wizard.
- LAWS: compliance strip "FENSA/Certass — certificate lodged with the council automatically" · guide pricing "windows from £X installed" · guarantee hero: years, coverage, transferability, IBG · deposit protection at quote CTA · design wizard (style→material→size→postcode) labelled estimate, measure-up step 2 · finance only with FRN + auto CONC 3 representative block · conservatories sold by style archetype with per-style price bands · dual route: instant estimate + free design appointment (incl. virtual).

## Swimming Pools
- SPATA is the only body; SPATASHIELD = £30k completion bond + £20k/3yr rectification warranty — the objection-killer at £50k–150k, but only full installer members carry it (case law: never imply cover not held).
- Compass Pools benchmark: brochure-by-post capture (6–18 month cycles), pool cost calculator, 20-yr warranty, named technology USP, press bar, named+photographed testimonials.
- LAWS: SPATASHIELD explained in pounds, gated on membership grade · dual CTA ladder (brochure pack ↔ design consultation), no instant-quote pretence · honest cost guide banded by type · build-story case studies (challenges, timeline, drone footage) · one named technical USP · warranty + running-cost transparency · named testimonials only.

## EV Charger Installation
- Grants: driveway homeowners INELIGIBLE; flats/renters 75% to £500 (to Mar 2027), landlords £500/socket — and the OZEV-authorised installer claims it for the customer. Charger must be on the OZEV approved list.
- Smart Home Charge benchmark: "from £382" price anchoring, photo-based remote survey (fusebox + parking photos) triaging standard vs non-standard, same-day quote, charger-review content hub as SEO moat.
- LAWS: fixed installed price per charger model · photo-based remote survey funnel, no default site visits · grant module targeted at flats/renters/landlords only ("we claim it for you") · charger comparison content hub · trust: NICEIC/NAPIT + OZEV + Trustpilot + warranty years · finance only with displayed FRN · secondary CTA = scheduled callback.

## Battery Storage (standalone)
- 0% VAT on home batteries (standalone included) until 31 Mar 2027 — legitimate deadline urgency, auto-expiring copy. MCS not legally required (matters for export tariffs); Flexi-Orb credible alternative mandating insurance-backed warranties.
- Lead engines: tariff-arbitrage payback calculators + product-anchored price pages (Powerwall 3 etc.). Under-served: "battery WITHOUT solar — is it worth it?" content.
- LAWS: 0% VAT deadline banner with auto-expiry · certification honesty (MCS/Flexi-Orb + one-line explainer, never "legally required") · payback calculator as primary capture, result emailed · dedicated battery-without-solar page · per-brand price pages "installed from £X" · case studies with kWh, tariff, before/after monthly bill, winter/summer split · warranty stack incl. cycles · savings flagged as tariff-dependent estimates.

# TRACK C — Outdoor / Visual Transformation Trades

## Landscaping (design & build)
- Trust hierarchy homeowners respond to: awards > manufacturer guarantee (Marshalls Register / Bradstone Assured 10-yr product+workmanship) > BALI/APL > insurance. 3D renders sell as risk-removal ("see your garden before we build it") — the forward-looking twin of before/after. Winter = design phase ("ready by spring, last-season rates"); spring = scarcity. Pricing: project bands (~£80–100/m² whole-garden), design fees 15–20%.
- LAWS: case studies with budget BANDS (nobody does this — pre-qualification edge) · mandatory 3D-render section with render-vs-finished pairs · named 5–6 step Design & Build journey; one positioning (D&B or build-only), never both · trust stack with a benefit sentence per badge + licensed-waste-carrier line · season-flip module · guide pricing as project bands, never day rates · element pages (patio/turf/decking/fencing) with per-m² prices linking to full D&B.

## Tree Surgery
- Credentials as verifiable facts: ArbAC licence NUMBER on homepage (Thor's Trees pattern), NPTC codes, "£5m public liability" stated as figure, waste-carrier number — mirrors what councils/police tell homeowners to verify. TPO/conservation-area handling ("we do the council application") is a genuine differentiator; 24/7 storm work is hero-level Oct–Feb.
- LAWS: "Check our credentials" block (numbers, not badges) · mandatory TPO/conservation page with timelines + penalty warning · 24/7 emergency channel, auto-elevated Oct–Feb · explicit anti-rogue pledge ("We never cold-call. Written quotes only.") + red-flags page · small/medium/large guide bands + free site visit caveats · domestic/commercial toggle · homepage FAQ = the four pre-call objections · "Established 19XX" + review volume in metrics band.

## Artificial Grass
- No industry body — manufacturer-led trust (approved installer + 10-yr product warranty) + awards/brand-client logos. Signature funnel: FREE Samples → FREE Survey two-rung ladder. Named tiered SKUs with pile height; pets/kids as named PRODUCTS not bullets; installed £45–80/m².
- LAWS: persistent FREE Samples CTA in header + sticky bar · two-step sample→survey funnel · 3-tier named product table (Budget/Family/Premium-Pet) with supply £/m² · pet-friendly = named product + dedicated page · paired warranty badges (product + installation) · mandatory sub-base cutaway diagram (price justification) · season-switch strip · trust row = client logos + Trustpilot, not certification badges.

## Tarmac & Surfacing
- No consumer accreditation — substitute insurance value as headline number ("£10m AXA PL"), Checkatrade, Companies House/VAT footer permanence signals. THE canonical rogue-trader trade ("leftover tarmac" scam): anti-rogue block is the highest-leverage element. Commercial split by service-type nav (Driveways | Car Parks | Line Marking); CHAS/Constructionline + fleet photos near-absent sector-wide = free wins.
- LAWS: dual audience nav with separate CTAs (Free Quote vs Site Visit/Tender Pack) · mandatory anti-rogue block on every driveway page (never cold-call, no leftover-tarmac deals, company + VAT numbers, £Xm insured) · auto-generate "rogue tarmac gangs" article (trust + SEO) · insurance figure as headline · commercial page: CHAS badges + fleet/plant photo slot · band + worked examples, never rate card · depth/spec cutaway diagram (scams skip the sub-base) · footer permanence signals incl. landline.

## Brickwork
- Runs on proof, not badges: "Est. 19XX" + reviews + "fully insured"; heritage niche = SPAB language + craftsman lineage. Expertise signals: naming pointing styles (weather-struck, flush, tuck…) with close-up photos; mortar/brick matching as pre-quote ritual. Nobody states a written guarantee — free differentiator.
- LAWS: evidence stack (years + reviews + insured; surface FMB/TrustMark hard if held) · "Pointing Styles" module with 5 named profiles · mortar & brick matching ritual section · geo-captioned before/afters cross-linked to SAPs · "from" job prices all-in, structural = price-on-survey · three tones: garden walls / repointing / structural · heritage sub-identity if lime-capable ("never cement on pre-1919 walls") · auto-insert 10-yr workmanship guarantee.

## Exterior Cleaning (jet wash / render / roof / softwash)
- Fragmented, low-regulation: SoftWash certification + COSHH/HSE-approved-biocide language is compliance AND marketing; render-manufacturer approval (K-Rend/Weber) is the render niche's best badge. "We NEVER pressure wash render/roofs" is the authority play. Regrowth guarantees (biocide lasts 2–4 yrs) almost universally unclaimed.
- LAWS: transformation-first hero (before/after IS the hero, labelled) · mandatory "why we never jet wash roofs & render" education block · badge hierarchy per surface page · ship a default written regrowth guarantee ("moss-free 3 years or we re-treat free") · size-banded fixed prices or instant calculator · page per surface with method mapping · seasonal: spring moss removal / autumn pre-winter biocide + re-treat reminders · driveway upsell ladder clean→re-sand→seal.

## Painting & Decorating
- Dulux Select = strongest mark (on-site assessment + 2-yr AkzoNobel-backed guarantee) — always translated into benefit words. Kitchen/uPVC spraying is a standalone high-margin niche: anchor vs REPLACEMENT (respray £1.5–7k vs £12–25k new kitchen, "70% saving"), sample door first, 10-yr coating guarantee. In-home trust protocol (DBS, uniforms, dust sheets, daily tidy) is a market-wide gap.
- LAWS: Dulux Select translated or own-brand written guarantee — never an empty slot · spraying pages standalone with replacement-vs-respray table + sample-door offer + separate form · "Living In Your Home" trust block as default · per-room fixed guide prices, never bare day rates · season-switch hero (exterior Feb–Aug, interior Sep–Jan) · portfolio = styled case studies with prep shots + products named · domestic/commercial split · name the paint brands.

# TRACK D — Booked Van Services

## Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning
- NCCA membership (insurance is a membership condition) + dedicated "why an NCCA member" page + link to member directory/ReferenceLine. Quote calculator = lead magnet not e-commerce (rooms + items + stairs → running total → "email me this quote"; minimum charge floor; "guide only"). Stain-CLUSTER pages (pet urine family, red wine, coffee) for motivated problem-first buyers.
- LAWS: NCCA hero chip + dedicated page + directory link · calculator ending in lead capture with min charge · named service tiers with inclusions · stain-cluster landing pages with honesty section ("can it be saved?") · upholstery/rug/mattress priced per item · drying time + method answered on page · commercial spur pages by property type.

## Domestic/Commercial Cleaning
- Recurring sold on the trio: same cleaner every visit + discounted regular rate + skip/cancel anytime; one-off deep/EOT clean is the acquisition product. Vetting formula: "DBS-checked · reference-vetted · fully insured (£Xm)" — never claim enhanced DBS for domestic. Commercial page = capability statement (CHAS/SafeContractor/BICSc/ISO, insurance limits, sector case studies, site-survey CTA).
- LAWS: split funnel at hero (home = book online; business = site survey) · recurring trio messaging · vetting block + "how we vet" expandable · numbered reclean guarantee ("free within 72 hours") · deep-clean/EOT pages feeding recurring upsell · commercial capability statement · keyholding + COSHH policy stated.

## House/Garage Clearance + Waste Removal
- NON-NEGOTIABLE: EA upper-tier waste carrier registration displayed as tier + full CBDU number + deep-link to the public register entry ("check us — and check anyone else"). Householder duty of care = they get fined if their waste is fly-tipped (up to £600 FPN, unlimited on conviction; S.C.R.A.P. code) — truthful fear positioning beside the quote CTA. AnyJunk benchmark: "98% diverted from landfill" + audit trail. Pricing by van-load fraction with visual estimator; photo-quote via WhatsApp primary funnel.
- LAWS: licence block footer + dedicated page with register deep-link · fly-tipping duty-of-care section beside CTA · "where your waste goes" page with real diversion % + waste transfer note on every job · van-load visual estimator + photo-quote funnel · distinct pages: probate/bereavement clearance (discretion, valuables set aside, donation receipts), hoarder, garage, single-item, vs-skip comparison · before/afters with load size + diversion outcome · optional scrap licence field, never claimed as required.

## Scaffolding
- Buyers verify: CISRS cards, TG20:21 sheets, SG4:22, RAMS, £10m PL, CHAS/SMAS/Constructionline; NASC = premium signal. Domestic sold on speed + tidiness; commercial on sectors + compliance pack. Case studies beat galleries for tenders.
- LAWS: dual-audience hero · compliance strip (CISRS · TG20:21/SG4:22 · £10m PL) with downloadable cert/sample RAMS · genuine badges only; NASC explainer if held · commercial case-study led (project, scaffold type, duration, main contractor) · domestic page answers pricing model (weekly hire + erect/dismantle), duration, liability · quote form with company + drawings upload · handover certificate + 7-day scafftag inspections stated.

## Mobile Mechanic / Garage (Clutch/Cambelt/Wetbelt) / MOT & Servicing
- ClickMechanic/Fixter funnel: reg + postcode → instant fixed price (manufacturer repair times + regional labour) → book → pay after work; 12-month/12,000-mile parts & labour warranty is the consumer expectation. Motor Ombudsman = only CTSI-approved code for service & repair. WETBELT = exploding content niche (Ford EcoBoost/PSA PureTech): symptom checklist, affected-models table with engine codes, honest interval advice, from-prices per model, consequence framing.
- LAWS: reg-plate lookup in hero feeding fixed-price menu · all-in pricing language ("no hidden extras") · 12-month/12k-mile warranty at every CTA · trust: TMO > Good Garage Scheme > IMI > DVSA MOT station, linked to registers · wetbelt/cambelt/clutch specialist pages (symptoms, models table, intervals, from-price, "failure costs 5–10x prevention") · mobile = "we come to you" + coverage checker; garage = collection & delivery / courtesy car · pay-after-work + spread-the-cost · post-job verified review loop.

## Car Detailing
- Premium visual trade: installer accreditations (XPEL/Stek/Gtechniq approved) are the trade's Gas Safe; package ladder (Maintenance Valet → Enhancement → Correction → Ceramic → PPF) with "from £X" + duration; WhatsApp photo-quote primary; facility trust (indoor studio, insured, CCTV) for £50k+ cars; founder-fronted video credibility.
- LAWS: image-first design with per-service and per-marque galleries · named package ladder with from-price + duration + inclusions · WhatsApp photo upload as primary funnel with condition caveat · approved-installer badges + manufacturer warranty lengths per package · facility trust block · founder/tech personality + process films · deposit-to-book with genuine scarcity calendar.

# TRACK E — Solicitors + Platform Layer

## Solicitors (heaviest-regulated vertical)
**SRA Transparency Rules 2018 — prices MUST be published for**: residential conveyancing, uncontested probate, immigration (ex-asylum), motoring offences (magistrates), employment tribunal claims (claimant + defending for businesses), debt recovery ≤£100k, business licensing. Each price page MUST show: total/range + charging basis, itemised disbursements, VAT as % or £, who does the work + supervisor (qualifications/experience), included/excluded services, key stages + timescales. Quote calculators MUST output a figure with ZERO contact-gating. Placement: prominent, signposted, easy to find.
**SRA digital badge**: mandatory since Nov 2019 — the LIVE Yoshki embed (registered via mySRA); a static image copy is NON-COMPLIANT. Footer strip: "Authorised and regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority — SRA No. X". Complaints page: firm procedure + Legal Ombudsman (8-week rule) + SRA route. Detect regulator first: CLC firms get the CLC Secure Badge instead — wrong badge is itself a breach. Enforcement: rolling SRA web sweeps, fines to £25,000 without tribunal.
**Conversion**: avg law-site conversion ~3.4%; fixed fees + instant quote calculators turn the compliance burden into the differentiator; triple CTA (phone / form / callback) + live chat; "free initial consultation" default; CQS near-mandatory for conveyancing (lender panels), Lexcel, Resolution; ReviewSolicitors + Google; real solicitor photos/bios (people hire people); homepage segments visitors into 2–4 matter paths (Maples pattern).
**LAWS**: structured price-page schema per in-scope service (fields, not free text) · who-does-the-work block · live SRA badge injection, never static · footer regulatory strip · complaints page linked footer-wide · zero contact-gating on calculators · plain-English practice-area pages · fixed-fee-led presentation · trust rail CQS/Lexcel + ReviewSolicitors · pre-publish "SRA lint" (badge live + SRA number + complaints + complete price fields) — publish blocked on failure.

## Platform layer (applies to all ~35 trades)
1. One design system, per-trade content SCHEMAS (structured fields rendered by shared components); regulatory fields schema-enforced.
2. Badge registry: scheme, number, expiry, official asset/embed, register deep-link; auto-removal on lapse; never recoloured/stretched; dynamic embeds preferred (SRA legally requires it).
3. Three quote-module archetypes (calculator / reg-lookup / photo-quote) configured per trade.
4. GBP category mapping table per trade (primary = most specific money keyword; up to 9 secondaries; 6-month re-audit; seasonal switching for HVAC).
5. Per-trade compliance linter at publish: machine-checkable MUSTs; no site publishes with a failed MUST.
6. Location-page uniqueness modules (reviews, staff, jobs, service-area data injected per town) — the proven anti-doorway pattern.
7. Finance-promotion gate built ONCE: FCA FRN present → CONC 3 representative example auto-inserted; absent → all monthly-payment copy stripped. Applies to windows, batteries, EV, pools, boilers, dental alike.
8. Regulated disclosure modules double as conversion features — build them as calculators and fee tables, not boilerplate.
